Why We Built KIDV Invoice
KIDV Tech was founded in Ahmedabad, Gujarat with one mission: make professional invoicing accessible to every Indian business owner. We saw small business owners struggling with complex accounting software, manual Excel sheets, and expensive CA bills just to create a simple GST invoice.

KIDV Invoice was born out of that frustration — a simple, affordable, fully GST-compliant invoicing tool that anyone can use without any accounting knowledge. From a freelance developer to a small textile trader in Surat, our platform serves businesses across India.
