1
infoInformation We Collect

When you use KIDV Invoice, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and password (encrypted by Firebase Auth).
  • Business Data: Company name, GSTIN, PAN, address, bank details that you enter in Settings.
  • Invoice Data: Invoices, quotations, payments, client records, and product/service lists that you create.
  • Client Data: Names, phone numbers, email addresses, GSTIN numbers of your clients.
  • Usage Data: Login timestamps and basic usage patterns (no tracking of specific actions).
  • Payment Data: Subscription plan details and payment status (actual payment processing is handled by Razorpay).
2
storageHow We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To Provide the Service: Generate GST invoices, manage clients, track payments, and produce reports.
  • Account Management: Create and manage your KIDV Invoice account, subscription, and settings.
  • Customer Support: Respond to your queries via WhatsApp, email, or phone.
  • Service Improvement: Understand how the app is used to fix bugs and add new features.
  • Legal Compliance: Maintain records as required under Indian GST and tax laws.

We do NOT use your data for advertising, profiling, or selling to third parties.

3
local_fire_departmentFirebase & Cloud Storage

KIDV Invoice uses Google Firebase (Firestore) for cloud data storage and Firebase Authentication for login. This means:

  • Your data is stored on Google's secure servers (data centers in Mumbai/Asia region).
  • All data transmission is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Firebase's security rules ensure only you can access your own data.
  • Google Firebase complies with GDPR, SOC 2, ISO 27001 and other international security standards.
warning_amber Firebase is a Google product. By using KIDV Invoice, your data is also subject to Google Firebase's Privacy Policy.
4
shareData Sharing

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data. We only share data in these limited situations:

  • Google Firebase: For cloud storage and authentication (as described above).
  • Razorpay: For payment processing. Only payment-related information is shared with Razorpay, subject to their privacy policy.
  • EmailJS: If you use the email notification feature, your subscription confirmation details may be processed by EmailJS.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by Indian law, court order, or government authority.
5
securityData Security

We implement multiple layers of security to protect your data:

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text — handled by Firebase Auth with industry-standard hashing.
  • Firestore Security Rules ensure each user can only access their own data.
  • Regular security reviews of Firestore rules and access policies.
  • Admin-only access for support operations, logged and audited.

However, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use a strong password and keep your login credentials confidential.

6
manage_accountsYour Rights

You have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access: View all your data at any time within the app.
  • Export: Download all your data as JSON from Settings → Data & Reset → Export JSON.
  • Delete: Delete individual records or clear all data from Settings → Data & Reset.
  • Account Deletion: Contact us at amit@kidvtech.com to permanently delete your account and all associated data.
  • Correction: Update your information at any time via Settings.
7
child_careChildren's Privacy

KIDV Invoice is a business tool intended for use by adults (18 years and older) and registered businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

If you believe that a child under 18 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at amit@kidvtech.com and we will delete such information.

8
cookieCookies & Local Storage

KIDV Invoice uses browser localStorage (not tracking cookies) to:

  • Cache your settings and data locally for faster loading.
  • Store your Firebase configuration.
  • Remember your invoice settings and preferences.

Firebase may set authentication session cookies. These are essential for the app to function and cannot be disabled. We do not use any advertising or tracking cookies.

updateChanges to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Notify you via the app or email if the changes are material.

Continued use of KIDV Invoice after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
