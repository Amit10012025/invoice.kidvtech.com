Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026 | KIDV Tech, Ahmedabad, Gujarat

1. Information We Collect

KIDV Invoice collects the following information when you use our service:

  • Account information: Name, email address, mobile number
  • Business information: Company name, GSTIN, address, bank details
  • Invoice data: Customer details, product/service details, transaction amounts
  • Usage data: Login times, features used, device information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the KIDV Invoice service
  • To generate GST-compliant invoices on your behalf
  • To send important account and subscription notifications
  • To improve our software and customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), hosted in Google's data centers. We implement industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, Firebase Authentication, and Row-Level Security to protect your information.

Your invoice and business data is private and accessible only to your account.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties, except:

  • Google Firebase (our cloud infrastructure provider)
  • When required by Indian law or court order
  • With your explicit consent

5. Your Rights

You have the right to: access your data, correct inaccurate data, delete your account and data, and export your invoice data. Contact us at support@kidvtech.com for any data requests.
